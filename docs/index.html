Associate Professor of Physics at COMSATS University Islamabad, working across advanced materials, printed and flexible electronics, sustainable technologies, research translation, and the responsible governance of emerging technologies.

Research → Technology

Connecting laboratory research with application

My work has moved between academic research, international collaborative programmes, industrial R&D and early-stage technology translation. This includes postdoctoral research at the University of Manchester's National Graphene Institute and subsequent industrial experience, alongside continuing work on scalable materials and device technologies.

QuantuMaTech is an independent technology-translation initiative through which I explore practical pathways for advanced materials, prototyping, scientific services and sustainable manufacturing concepts.

Leadership & governance

Scientific judgement beyond the laboratory

Alongside research and teaching, I am interested in how universities and research organisations evaluate evidence, govern emerging technologies, build research capability and translate technical work responsibly. My developing work in AI and technology governance is an extension of this trajectory rather than a claim of established AI-policy specialization.

Positioning principle: evidence evaluation, experimental validation, technology assessment and institutional decision-making provide the bridge between my established scientific work and emerging governance questions.
